Press and drag on the glass to breathe: warm breath melts the ice back and the frost there retreats. Double-tap to start a new crystal. Each crystal gives off heat as it freezes, so it soon runs out of cold and only grows toward glass that is still undercooled — that is why fronts flatten where crystals meet.
