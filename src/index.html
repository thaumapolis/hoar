Press and drag on the glass to breathe: warm water blots out the cold, and the frost there stops growing. Double-tap to start a new crystal. Each crystal gives off heat as it freezes, so it soon runs out of fuel and only grows toward undercooled glass — that is why the fronts flatten where crystals meet.
